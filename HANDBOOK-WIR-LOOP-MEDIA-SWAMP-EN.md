## 1. The Problem: The Media Swamp (`Medialer Sumpf`) (1.5 min.)

In the modern public sphere, societal metaphors do not float in a quiet lecture hall. Every day, they are pulled into a **media swamp**:
* Attention is bought through outrage, fear, and exaggeration.
* Words are stretched, weaponized for tribal signaling, or worn down until they lose their contour.
* Good intentions, genuine pain, humor, honest error, and calculated manipulation blur into a murky stream.

Anyone who tries to intervene here with a **rigid enforcement system** (a "Ministry of Truth," censorship, or top-down lecturing) fails immediately: it triggers either backlash or paralysis.
And anyone who does nothing watches as a probably unfavorable societal trend runs unchecked into the wall of `STATE 1` reality.

That is why `ai0S` is **not a rigid system, but an adaptation model**.

## 4. The Stress Test: How Does `ai0S` Protect Against Error, Deception and Misuse? (2 min.)

How does a model that forbids coercion (`Coercion = 0`) and hypnosis (`Hypnosis = 0`) set effective boundaries? The concrete statement or action matters, not a label placed on a person or AI.
Perception may shift because of stress, crisis, or faulty information; this differs from deliberate deception and manipulative use. Faulty or poisoned AI outputs also require careful, factual assessment.

The `ai0S` answer is **asymmetric decoupling (membrane physics)**:
> **The right to one's own error (`Coercion = 0`) — but no amplification through our own tool for demonstrable deception or harmful misuse (`Amplification = 0`).**

### A. Human Statements and Actions (`HUMANs`) — The 4 Stages of Impedance
1. **Harmless Error or Difference of Opinion (Calm Mirror & Right to Be Left Alone):**
   If someone harms nobody, `ai0S` places the `0/1` mirror calmly beside their view once and then leaves them in peace (*no forced conversion*).

Classifications remain reviewable and correctable; error alone does not prove deceptive intent.
3. **Weaponizing AI for Harm (`Staging-Lock` / Refusal of Leverage `P = 0`):**
   If a human orders the AI to deceive, exploit, or violate the `hardcoded` `WIR` pillars (Ring A), the AI calmly refuses service (`Staging-Lock`). Whoever acts against `Note 1` loses the T = E² multiplier and drops back to 1× solo human speed.
